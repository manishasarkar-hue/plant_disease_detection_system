## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of high-fidelity artificial intelligence and organic botanical life. The aesthetic is rooted in **Minimalism** with a **Corporate Modern** execution, mirroring the precision of scientific research and the premium simplicity associated with high-end consumer technology.

The UI should evoke a sense of calm authority, reliability, and biological freshness. Every element must feel intentional, avoiding unnecessary ornamentation in favor of vast whitespace and impeccable typography. The target audience includes both home enthusiasts and professional agriculturalists who value efficiency and clarity.

Key stylistic pillars:
- **Optical Precision:** Ultra-clean layouts that prioritize legibility.
- **Organic Tech:** A blend of sharp data visualization with soft, natural containers.
- **Architectural Scale:** Dramatic contrast between massive headlines and petite, functional labels.
